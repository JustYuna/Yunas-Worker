# Privacy Policy - Yeco
> Last Updated: June 28 2026
> Support Server: [https://discord.gg/sPQFM9Rj33](https://discord.gg/sPQFM9Rj33)

Yeco respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about the data we process. By using the bot, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

---

## 1. Data Collection
To provide economy and leveling features, Yeco stores the following data:
* **User Identifiers:** Discord User IDs (to link data to your account).
* **Economy Data:** Balances (Main/Second currency), transaction history, and deposited amounts.
* **Gameplay Statistics:** Level/XP, gambling history, robbery stats, and upgrade levels.
* **Interaction Data:** Daily streaks, vote timestamps, and command usage counts.
* **Settings:** User preferences, such as leaderboard visibility status.

**Note:** We do **not** collect sensitive personal data such as passwords, private messages, or personal account credentials.

---

## 2. Data Storage & Usage

**Storage**
All user data is stored locally using **Sqlite3**. This data is kept on our private hosting infrastructure and is not accessible to unauthorized third parties.

**Usage**
Collected data is used strictly for:
* Economy system functionality and command processing.
* Displaying global statistics (e.g., leaderboards or public profiles).
* Preventing abuse of internal systems and ensuring fair gameplay.
* Debugging technical issues, maintaining system stability, and restoring corrupted or missing user data.

Global visibility can be toggled via `/settings <Leaderboard Visibility>`.

---

## 3. Sharing & Third Parties
We do **not** sell, trade, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only accessed by the bot's developers for maintenance or shared if strictly required by law enforcement or to comply with Discord’s Terms of Service.

---

## 4. Removal & Retention
**Your Rights**
Users can delete their own data at any time using the `/me <Delete Data>` command.
> **Note:** This will delete all your data permanently without backups. Once deleted, your progress cannot be recovered.

**Retention**
We retain data only as long as you are an active user of the bot. Data associated with accounts that have been inactive for an extended period may be purged to ensure data minimization.

This privacy policy may be updated at any time to reflect bot changes, feature additions, or legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
